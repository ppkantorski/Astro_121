// Notes on coordinate systems:

Main types of coordinate systems-
   1. Galactic (won't be used)
   2. Equitorial (RA, dec)
	z (up) = north pole (Polaris)
	y = east
	x = radially out from pole
   3. Topocentric (altitude, azimuth)
	y = north
	z = up from your position on Earth
	x = east
	
   For convention, use (RA, dec) (an analogous to lattitude/longitude) for equitorial coordinates.

Topocentric-
   Azimuth = start at north (y), rotate clockwise to measure
   Altitude = go upwards
   Nice and simple for telescope positioning.

Sidereal Time-
   Sun returns to the same position on earth after 24h.
   However, the earth is also in motion around the sun.
   This time difference between the rotation and motion is called severial time.
   This is why actual days are slighlty shorter than 24h, ~23.98h or 86164 seconds. (password in astronomy!)

Precession/Nutation-
   Coordinates given for a particular epoch.
   They need to precess within the current epoch (precessing your coordinates).
   Reason for the seasons on earth.
